Community Service Project

Our CSP journey

Awareness of Central Government Schemes and the Level of Participation — a project by CSE students studying how well people know about, and take part in, Central Government schemes.

Location

Chevuturu, Andhra Pradesh, India

Duration

13 May 2026 – 22 June 2026

Faculty guide

Dr. G. Sambi Reddy

Associate Professor, Department of CSE

Problem statement & objectives

Many people are unaware of the schemes available to them. Even when they know a scheme exists, understanding its benefits, eligibility, application process and documents can be hard. The project looks at:

Awareness of government schemes
Participation and accessibility
Lack of information
Document-related difficulties
Lack of digital knowledge

Survey findings

Awareness levels

Survey
sample
Fully aware 30%Partially 38%Not aware 32%

Major barriers

Challenges identified

  • Lack of information (62%)
  • Lack of documents (55%)
  • Lack of digital knowledge (54%)

Proposed solutions

Recommendations only — not completed activities.

  • Local-language pamphlets
  • Digital literacy workshops
  • Document assistance
  • WhatsApp information groups
  • Panchayat facilitation support
  • Digital kiosks

Team

Conclusion

The survey suggests that awareness is only part of the picture: most respondents were not fully aware of schemes, and information, documents and digital skills were all frequently reported barriers. Clear, local, accessible information can help people take the first step.

About

Why SchemeSahay exists

SchemeSahay was created from our Community Service Project in Chevuturu, after seeing that many people struggle to find clear, simple information about Central Government schemes.

The awareness gap

Only 30% of surveyed respondents were fully aware of schemes; 38% were partially aware and 32% not aware.

Challenges for citizens

Lack of information (62%), lack of documents (55%) and limited digital knowledge (54%).

How the platform helps

One place to explore schemes, see preliminary pointers, prepare documents and reach official portals.

Proposed improvements

These are recommendations for the community, not completed activities.

📄 Local-language pamphlets
💻 Digital literacy workshops
🗂 Document assistance
💬 WhatsApp information groups
🏛 Panchayat facilitation support
🖥 Digital kiosks

Expected community impact

If adopted, these steps could help more people understand schemes, prepare documents earlier and approach official channels with confidence. Actual impact would need to be measured.
